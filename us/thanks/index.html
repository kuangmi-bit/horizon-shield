Thank you. PayPal has your payment, and we have your order (see your email).

What happens next

  1. We read your quote or description and match each line to U.S. public cost data.
  2. A person checks the draft before it leaves us.
  3. We email your documents within one business day for a Quote Check, and within two business days for a Detailed Estimate. A business day is Monday to Friday, except U.S. federal holidays.

If something is wrong

If we cannot read your file or find no usable public data for your trade and ZIP code, we tell you and refund the full price. If you do not see our email within the time above, check your spam folder, then write to contact@the-horizons-innovation.com with your order number.
