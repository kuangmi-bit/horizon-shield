Accessibility Statement

Last updated September 27, 2026
  1. What we do
  2. How we check
  3. Known limitations
  4. Another way to use the Service
  5. Feedback

We want everyone to be able to check a quote. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA.

What we do

  • Semantic HTML with headings, landmarks, labeled form fields and a skip link
  • Text and interface contrast at or above WCAG AA ratios, in both light and dark themes that follow your device setting
  • Every function usable with a keyboard, with a visible focus indicator
  • No content that flashes, and reduced motion when your device asks for it
  • Tables with header cells, and numbers set so screen readers read them as numbers

How we check

Last reviewed September 27, 2026, with automated checks and a manual review using only a keyboard, at desktop and phone widths. We review again whenever we change a page template.

Known limitations

  • Wide tables in reports scroll sideways on small screens.
  • Photos of paper quotes can be hard for AI to read; typed or PDF quotes work best.

Another way to use the Service

If anything on the site gets in your way, email your quote and ZIP code to contact@the-horizons-innovation.com. We will prepare the same Report at the same price and send it in a format that works for you, such as plain text or an accessible PDF.

Feedback

Tell us about any barrier at contact@the-horizons-innovation.com. We reply within two business days and tell you what we will fix and when.
