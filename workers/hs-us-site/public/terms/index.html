1. What the Service is

The Service has three parts: a free Quick Estimate, a Quote Check that compares the lines of one contractor quote you provide with U.S. public cost data, and a Detailed Estimate built from the measurements, photos and description you provide. Each returns a written report (the "Report"): reference values, their sources and formulas, questions you may ask, and a SHA-256 receipt. A Report is price benchmarking information for your own decisions.

The free Quote Teardown is separate from the three parts above. It lists the lines of a quote you send, marks lines that cannot be checked, writes questions you may ask and, when you give a ZIP code, shows public wage statistics for the trade. It is produced automatically, is not reviewed by a person and is not a Report.
