What we are

HORIZON SHIELD is a buyer-side price benchmarking service. We are paid only by homeowners and take no fees from contractors, suppliers or insurers. HORIZON SHIELD provides price benchmarking information only. We are not a licensed contractor, engineer, architect, home inspector, public adjuster, appraiser or attorney. We do not inspect property, assess its condition or code compliance, prepare or negotiate insurance claims, bid on, arrange or supervise work, or give legal advice.
